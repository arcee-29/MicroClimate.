Rain gauge information

  • Each bucket tip produces a pulse counted by the ESP32.
  • Rainfall in millimeters depends on the gauge's calibrated mm-per-tip value.
  • Rainfall intensity is calculated over a rolling 60-minute period.
  • Keep the collector level and clear of leaves or debris.

Sensor installation

  • Use a calibrated pyranometer if the study claims measurement in W/m².
  • Mount the sensor horizontally with an unobstructed view of the sky.
  • Follow the sensor datasheet for analog, RS485, or other interface wiring.
  • Protect connectors from water while keeping the sensing surface exposed.

Reading guide

Solar radiation changes with cloud cover, time of day, shading, and sensor orientation.

A BH1750 or VEML7700 measures illuminance in lux, not solar irradiance directly. Do not label lux readings as W/m² without a validated calibration method.
